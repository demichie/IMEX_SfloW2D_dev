# Binary restart v5 and diagnostic CSV verification

This report records the focused verification of restartable runout diagnostics
and controlled continuation of the runout and mass-centre CSVs, plus the
completed ETNA/ETNA_LAVA continuation matrix and stochastic restart regression.
It does not claim closure of the broader N9 scientific acceptance gate.

## Implemented behavior

Version 5 checkpoints preserve the runout reference and initial extents,
previous barycentre and sampling history, maximum runout, and steady-stop
request/deadline. Binary v4 checkpoints are rejected because they lack the
history needed for a guaranteed diagnostic continuation. ASCII initial
conditions are not treated as binary restarts.

The runout CSV remains the only runout result file; the mass-centre CSV schema
is unchanged. Runtime code no longer reads or writes `runout.txt`. For binary
continuation, both existing CSV histories are checked against the restored
diagnostic state before either file is opened. Compatible files are appended
without duplicating the final sample. Missing files start new headered
segments. Invalid, incomplete or future histories are rejected without
modifying the files or checkpoint.

## Verified cases

The focused runner
`TESTS/TEST_BINARY_RESTART/test_runout_restart.py` passed all 18 checks with the
historical optimized executable:

- automatic and explicit runout origins, with exact checkpoint, physical
  output and complete CSV equality between uninterrupted and split runs;
- both CSVs missing and one CSV missing, with new segment headers and the
  expected continuation rows;
- rejection without modifying the CSVs or checkpoint for future, missing,
  mismatched-tail, incompatible-header, duplicate, truncated, non-finite,
  overflow and empty histories;
- rejection before opening CSVs when the origin, runout cadence or stopping
  threshold differs;
- explicit rejection of v4 checkpoints; and
- restoration of a positive-`EPS_STOP` steady-stop deadline.

The evolving-topography binary restart check also passed: the uninterrupted and
split runs produced byte-identical final checkpoints and solution snapshots.
The official `TEST_STOCHASTIC_RESTART` also passed with the v5 executables in
both build profiles and with actual one- and four-thread teams. The test
now explicitly disables serial mode and checks the solver-reported thread
count for the uninterrupted, first-leg and resumed runs. It confirmed
bit-for-bit equality of the full and split stochastic trajectories, including
the evolved stochastic variable. Per-run executable, checkpoint, snapshot and
log hashes are included in the v5 evidence manifest.

The focused runout test output and inputs are retained locally under
`/tmp/imex-restart-v5-runout-20261011-1357` and

## ETNA and ETNA_LAVA continuation matrix

The v5 matrix completed successfully for ETNA (200 s) and ETNA_LAVA (3600 s)
with strict/debug and historical optimized executables and actual OpenMP teams
of one and four threads: **8/8 runs passed**. The historical optimized and
strict/debug executables were reused from the retained acceptance builds. The
ETNA preprocessed rasters were reused after checksum verification. The runner
used NumPy 2.0.2 from the `corso_geo` environment.

For all eight cases:

- uninterrupted and split/restarted native NetCDF fields are exactly equal;
- the native fields are exactly equal to the pre-change physical baseline;
- final v5 checkpoints match between uninterrupted and split runs, and their
  canonical physical records match the pre-change checkpoints;
- the complete runout and mass-centre CSVs, including headers, are byte-for-byte
  equal between uninterrupted and split runs and to the pre-change full-run
  CSVs; and
- all compared ESRI outputs match both the split trajectory and the pre-change
  baseline.

All four within-build one-versus-four-thread pairs passed. No solver tolerance
was changed. The versioned machine-readable evidence is
`results_n9_restart_v5_2026_10_11.json`. Full and split raw run directories were
retained locally at `/tmp/imex-n9-restart-v5-20261011`; their individual hashes
and run metadata are recorded in the evidence manifest, but the bulky payloads
are not committed.

and is not reproduced by the official stochastic restart regression; it is not
evidence that stochastic restart coverage is missing. The machine-readable

one/four-thread comparison policy are retained; no solver tolerance or new
residual threshold is introduced. Bulky raw run payloads need not be retained;
the versioned evidence manifest preserves checksums and run metadata. The
approval is recorded against solver revision
`3f7329c85d2e30e33cf68348721329f7726b01c1` in
`results_n9_restart_v5_2026_10_11.json`. It applies only to that revision and
the scope stated above.
