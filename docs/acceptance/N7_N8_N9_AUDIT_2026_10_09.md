# IMEX_SfloW2D acceptance audit for N7 N8 and N9

The [Froude friction follow-up](FROUDE_FRICTION_FIX_2026_10_09.md) resolves the
strict stochastic failure. The subsequent
[N8 mass-exchange validation](N8_MASS_EXCHANGE_2026_10_09.md) adds isolated
production inventory, bed-volume, geometry and restart tests and corrects five
exchange defects. Both candidates pass their complete audits; the results below remain
the historical evidence for the pinned `752b47fe` baseline; N9 remains open for
the other acceptance conditions.

backend, but implementation, successful regression cases and milestone
acceptance are different claims. Remaining requirements include complete
production residual/CFL comparisons, variable-composition dynamics and isolated
mass-exchange inventory tests. The complete stochastic restart fails in the
strict floating-point profile, although it passes in the historical optimized
profile.

`752b47fe853f68798d2ec84ca4cb905a53e24300` and the operative R1–R11 revision of
`IMEX_SfloW2D_Development_Roadmap_2026-10-09_Updated.docx`. Historical KT status

## Execution scope

The audit exports only tracked files at the pinned solver SHA, regenerates
Autotools files in that export, and performs clean parallel out-of-tree builds.
Builds, executables, inputs and full logs remain outside the working repository
in `/tmp/imex-acceptance-752b47fe-20261009-final`.

The production profiles are `strict_debug`
(`-O0 -g -fcheck=all -fbacktrace -ffpe-trap=invalid,zero,overflow`) and
`historical_optimized` (`-Ofast -funroll-all-loops`). Different profiles are not
required to produce identical output bytes: their scientific checks retain the
original per-test limits.

The adapter sets `SERIAL_FLAG=F` only in temporary fixture inputs, records both
original/effective inputs and checks the team's actual size. This execution
override leaves the physical fixture and reference tolerances unchanged.
Conservative-field parsing accepts Fortran `D` exponents and an omitted `E` in
three-digit exponents without changing the output bytes.

The [criterion definitions](../../TESTS/ACCEPTANCE/acceptance_plan.json) retain
the complete uncovered portions. The [audit README](../../TESTS/ACCEPTANCE/README.md)
provides reproduction commands and retention rules. Raw output payloads are not
all committed; approved output archival remains part of the N9 closure.

at `5e-10`, uphill mass fraction at `1e-14`, G=1 lateral symmetry at `5e-10`, and
mode-specific partitions/fingerprints in
[`analyze_case.py`](../../TESTS/TEST_PCCU_GATE_H/analyze_case.py).

The two dry-safe Python core hashes match the available September 26 package and
the pinned Gate-H README. The external full Python suite has not been rerun in
this step; aggregate metrics do not replace full field/residual comparisons.

## Strict stochastic failure

The strict executable reports actual teams of 1 and 4, completes stochastic
initialization, then terminates with `SIGILL` before the first checkpoint. The
profile enables invalid/divide-by-zero/overflow traps. This audit does not yet
identify the exact offending operation or classify the signal as a physical
instability, benign intermediate arithmetic or toolchain behavior.

The corresponding optimized trajectories reach 3 s; uninterrupted/restarted
checkpoints and final q snapshots match exactly. Both results must be retained:
optimized success does not prove validity under the strict contract.

The full failing solver logs are stored alongside this report as
[one-thread failure](strict_stochastic_1.log) and
[four-thread failure](strict_stochastic_4.log). Effective input, seed, captured
output hashes and full build/invocation provenance are recorded in the JSON
results and in the retained external audit directory. The
[effective failing input](strict_stochastic.inp) is also saved; its initial
conservative file is generated by the pinned stochastic restart test, so this
input is not a standalone simulation directory.
