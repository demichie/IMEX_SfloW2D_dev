## Corrections and caveats

- `EXAMPLE_BW2D`: eight script arguments; conflicting pulse narratives not silently erased.
- `EXAMPLE_LATERAL_SOURCE`: the third argument is `MFR_SOURCE` in kg/s and the sixth is written to `XS_SOURCE` as a mass fraction. The former 900 K invocation with `XS_SOURCE=0.01` gives reduced gravity `-19.33 m/s²`; the mass-flow/Richardson-number thickness formula would take `sqrt(RI_SOURCE / reduced_gravity)`. The solver now rejects non-positive reduced gravity when a Richardson number is prescribed, before the square root or output creation. The active guide uses the user-approved 300 K source at the 300 K ambient temperature. At the documented pressure and 1% solids, the neutral-density threshold is approximately 303.03 K; at 300 K, source density is about 1.188 kg/m³ versus ambient 1.176 kg/m³, with reduced gravity about 0.098 m/s². The fixed boundary trace allows this run to reach 100 s with nonzero mass.
- `EXAMPLE_ENGWELL2016`: the sixth generator argument is written to `XS_SOURCE` as a mass fraction; the legacy volume-fraction description is incorrect.
- `EXAMPLE_DAMBREAK`: current generator is `create_dambreak.py`; historical filenames are identified as such. The 16 October failures at pore-pressure fractions 1.0 and 0.5 (about 0.36 s and 0.49 s) are superseded by the 20 October standard-template run, which reached 3 s with stable mass on the updated solver. The earlier diagnosis remains historical: fraction 1.0 canceled the initial `rho_m * g_red * h` vertical stress, and the solver clamped effective stress to zero. The tracked template's momentum `FLAG=1` is outflow-only in the current solver; it does not impose the impermeable side walls/no-flux right boundary claimed by the legacy README. The generator prints full hydrostatic excess pressure before applying the requested fraction.
- `EXAMPLE_ETNA`: `cleanFolder.sh` capitalization and ellipsoid-preprocessing explanation.
- `EXAMPLE_2D`: old `create_example4.py` is not the committed generator filename. The current generator needs NumPy and only imports Matplotlib when plotting is requested; it does not use the Basemap dependency listed by the legacy README. Its generated input enables both NetCDF and ESRI output.
- `EXAMPLE_ENSEMBLE_ETNA`: placeholder `ENSEMBLE_`, link/template execution stages retained.
- `EXAMPLE_CF`, `EXAMPLE_TAAL` and `EXAMPLE_TAAL_BOTTOM`: their committed namelists have ESRI output disabled, contrary to the generic legacy output descriptions.
- `EXAMPLE_CF`: an earlier extracted run reached a displayed time of 105.584 s, with `dt` rounded to 0.00000 and no visible time advance during the final several minutes. It was stopped after about 9 minutes; the later 20 October run reached the configured end time and supersedes this observation.
- `EXAMPLE_TAAL_BOTTOM`: because `BOTTOM_RADIAL_SOURCE_FLAG=T`, `TIME_PARAM(4)` is a phase offset, not a total source duration or cut-off. Its current value (150 s) is five 30 s periods and therefore gives no net phase shift. This differs from lateral `EXAMPLE_TAAL`, where `TIME_PARAM(4)` is an absolute cut-off.
- `EXAMPLE_ETNA_LAVA`: extra DEM and acceptance context restored; validation relates to a pinned source and input configuration.

The interface fix now preserves the prescribed depth for wave speeds, advective flux and the hydrostatic/path state. Inspection of all four orientations also exposed two coupled y-side issues: south/north boundary reconstruction did not restore the prescribed source trace, and the north source geometry was assigned to the south edge. Both were corrected.
