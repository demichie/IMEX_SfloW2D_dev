# N7 dynamic geometry validation

This test exercises finite-time HP-PCCU evolution on rough inclined beds,
near-dry fronts, rotated one-cell ramps and mixed-curvature topography. It
compares raw stages and every final conservative cell with an independently
assembled current-policy IMEX reference. A passing aggregate mass or a
matching output hash alone is not field agreement.

## Fixed case inventory

Each compiler profile runs 69 cases with actual one- and four-thread teams:

| Cases | Count | Comparison time |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Seeded rough, drainage, compact and inclined compact fronts | 24 | 0.02 s |
| Mixed Hessian dynamics and lakes, all slope and curvature toggles | 24 | 0.02 s |
| Axial and oblique one-cell ramps and their quarter-turn rotations | 12 | 0.02 s |
| Circular dry-front expansion on three grids | 9 | 0.1 s |

All supported stage counts are included: `N_RK=2,3,4`. Production CFL is
checked, not changed. Raw negativity, final repairs, mass budgets and
complete thread payloads are checked separately. The spatial observer also
checks active normal and tangential face momenta, thickness, free surface,
thermal and density closure and characteristic speeds at every raw solved
stage of the final step.

## Independent reference and policy differences

The clean Python spatial kernels retain their original checksums. Explicit
cache adapters implement the current production near-dry normal-momentum
bounds, all-derivative LS boundary copying, exact-rest roundoff policy and
final external ghost closure. These differences are specified in
`reference_adapter_contract_v2.json`; this comparator is not mislabeled as
the unchanged historical SSPRK2 algorithm. Gate-H checks that historical
trajectory separately, using the explicitly approved historical limits.

No production trajectory is used as a golden field. Per-step raw reference
admissibility is checked before optional prototype sanitization could hide
a negative state. Positive cell mass is retained until the production
machine-epsilon cleanup, independently of the face dry threshold.

## Versioned test corrections

`contract.json` retains the original fixture definition. Its mixed case
allowed outgoing mass to reach the open boundary; `contract_v2.json`
extends only the quiet collar, without relaxing budget or field limits.

`contract_v3.json` uses the user-approved refinement measurement for circular
isotropy: the absolute change of the signed fourfold moment from initial to
final time. The initial grid sampling can otherwise compensate the angular
distortion generated by evolution. The absolute final-anisotropy bound is
unchanged. The failed final-only refinement measurements remain recorded,
and every result retains signed initial and final moments.

`contract_v4.json` records the authorized reconstruction correction: centre
auxiliary velocities are zero for depth at or below the existing dry
threshold. Conservative cell fields and direct volumetric candidates remain
unchanged. This prevents negligible residual mass in a nominally dry neighbour
from altering the wet neighbour's auxiliary reconstruction.

`contract_v5.json` additionally records the user-authorized
curvature source guard at the same threshold. The local quadratic force is
evaluated only in resolved wet cells, without discarding mass or disabling
other sources. The reference selects wet cells before contracting velocities;
the historical SSPRK2 comparison still uses the original clean kernels.
Neither correction changes the CFL, the dry threshold or numerical limits.

The current `contract_v6.json` records the additionally authorized projection
of accepted conservative states: after observing and checking the raw final
IMEX assembly, set only the two momenta to zero for depth at or below the
shared dry threshold. Mass, thermal energy and transported components remain
unchanged. Raw stage states and the implicit solve are not projected. The
independent current-policy reference applies the same final-only mapping and
checks its complete predicted repair, without changing the historical core.

## Run and evidence

Run `sh TESTS/TEST_N7_GEOMETRY_DYNAMIC/run_test.sh`. Strict and optimized
production modules are built independently in temporary directories.
`KEEP_TEST_WORKDIR=1` retains complete fields, observer payloads and
`strict/evidence.json` and `optimized/evidence.json`.

The script checks both profiles even if the refinement assessment fails in
one. A failed assessment still writes its evidence and returns nonzero;
collecting all cases is not an acceptance waiver.
